The Problem

Slow Response Kills Roofing Leads.

When homeowners are dealing with leaks, storm damage, urgent repairs, or insurance questions, they do not wait around. They call whoever responds first.

📵

Missed Calls After Hours

Storm calls, leak calls, and urgent repair inquiries often happen when your team is on a roof, driving, or off the clock. Every unanswered call is a potential job that goes to a competitor.

⛈️

Storm Leads Pile Up

When demand spikes after weather events, unorganized lead handling causes good opportunities to slip away. The roofer with the faster system wins the inspection.

⏳

Web Forms Sit Too Long

A homeowner who fills out a form expects fast confirmation. Delayed response gives competitors room to win the estimate before you even follow up.

📋

Estimates Get Delayed

Slow inspection scheduling and delayed estimate follow-up reduce close rates and damage trust with homeowners who are comparing multiple roofers at once.

🔧

Manual Follow-Up Creates Leakage

Without automation, calls, forms, missed leads, estimate follow-ups, and old opportunities fall through the cracks. Revenue leaks silently every week.

Fewer inspections. Lower close rates. Lost revenue.
