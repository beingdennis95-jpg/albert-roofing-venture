# Research Notes — Roofing Industry Pain Points

These carousel themes were built around common roofing-industry growth and conversion pain points:

1. **Speed to lead matters**  
   Homeowners contacting roofers after storm damage, leaks, or urgent repair needs often reach out to multiple companies quickly. Slow response, missed calls, and delayed estimates reduce booked inspections and close rates.

2. **Trust is built before the first conversation**  
   Reviews, visual proof of work, licenses/insurance clarity, and a strong website influence whether a homeowner contacts a roofer in the first place.

3. **Feast-or-famine pipelines are common**  
   Many roofing businesses rely heavily on referrals or seasonal demand spikes. Without consistent lead generation, nurture, and reactivation systems, calendars become unpredictable.

4. **Manual follow-up creates leakage**  
   Web inquiries, missed calls, appointment scheduling, estimate follow-ups, and old lead reactivation often fall through without automation and CRM support.

5. **Marketing spend often lacks tracking**  
   Ads and outreach are harder to scale when there is no connected system for qualification, follow-up, pipeline visibility, and ROI tracking.
